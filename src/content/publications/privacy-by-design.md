## Abstract

Young people are increasingly aware of digital privacy risks. This blog discusses how privacy by design can empower youth to take control of their data and make informed choices online.

## Introduction

Privacy by design is a proactive approach to protecting user data from the outset.

## Methods

We surveyed high school students about their privacy concerns and digital habits.

## Results

Most youth value privacy but feel they lack tools to manage it effectively.

## Discussion

Education and user-friendly privacy settings are key to empowering youth.

## Conclusion

Privacy by design should be a standard in all digital products for young people.

## Abstract

This article explores the ethical considerations of using artificial intelligence in educational settings. It highlights the importance of transparency, fairness, and student privacy when deploying AI-powered tools in classrooms.

## Introduction

AI is transforming education, but ethical challenges must be addressed to ensure positive outcomes for all learners.

## Methods

We conducted interviews with educators and reviewed recent literature on AI in education.

## Results

Key findings include the need for clear data policies and ongoing teacher training.

## Discussion

Ethical frameworks can help guide responsible AI adoption in schools.

## Conclusion

AI in education offers great promise, but must be implemented with care and oversight.
